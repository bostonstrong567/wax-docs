The [Mods page](/mods) lists the mods made for Wax. You can search it, filter it by category and tag, and open
a mod to read what it does.

## Add to game

Press **Add to game** on a mod. Your browser asks if it may open Wax. Say yes.

Wax then downloads the mod, checks it and puts it in your game's mods folder.

- If ICARUS is running, the mod loads at once and a notice appears in the game.
- If ICARUS is not running, a small window tells you the mod was added. It is there the next time you start
  the game.

The button works on a PC where Wax was installed with `Install Wax.cmd`. If nothing happens when you press it,
use the download below.

## Download a zip

Press **Download zip** on a mod. Extract the zip into the mods folder of Wax:

```text
...\steamapps\common\Icarus\Icarus\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\Wax\mods\
```

The zip holds one folder, named after the mod. That folder goes straight into `mods`. When the game is running,
the mod shows up on the **Mods** page of the Wax menu, switched off. Switch it on there.

## Manage your mods

Open the Wax menu (F8) and go to **Mods**. Each mod has a card:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enabled** | Switches the mod on or off. It stays that way after you restart the game. |
| **Reload** | Loads the mod again. |
| Up and down arrows | Move the mod in the list. Mods load in this order. |
| Bin | Removes the mod. Wax asks first. |

A removed mod is not erased. Its folder is renamed so that it starts with `.removed-`, and Wax leaves folders
with such names alone. To get the mod back, rename the folder to the mod's name again.

## Updating a mod

Press **Add to game** again, or extract the new zip over the old folder. With the button, the old copy is kept
under a `.removed-` name.

The [Mods page](/mods) lists the mods made for Wax. You can search it, filter it by category and tag, and open
a mod to read what it does. Each card shows the mod's version and when it was last updated.

## Add to game

Press **Add to game** on a mod. Your browser asks if it may open Wax. Say yes.

Wax then downloads the mod, checks it and puts it in your game's mods folder.

- If ICARUS is running, the mod loads at once and a notice appears in the game.
- If ICARUS is not running, a small window tells you the mod was added. It is there the next time you start
  the game.

The button works on a PC where Wax was installed with `Install Wax.cmd`. If nothing happens when you press it,
use the download below.

## Download a zip

Press **Download zip** on a mod. Extract the zip into the mods folder of Wax:

```text
...\steamapps\common\Icarus\Icarus\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\Wax\mods\
```

The zip holds one folder, named after the mod. That folder goes straight into `mods`. When the game is running,
the mod shows up on the **Mods** page of the Wax menu, switched off. Switch it on there.

## Manage your mods

Open the Wax menu (F8) and go to **Mods**. Each mod has a card:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enabled** | Switches the mod on or off. It stays that way after you restart the game. |
| **Reload** | Loads the mod again. |
| Up and down arrows | Move the mod in the list. Mods load in this order. |
| Bin | Removes the mod. Wax asks first. |

A removed mod is not erased. Its folder is renamed so that it starts with `.removed-`, and Wax leaves folders
with such names alone. To get the mod back, rename the folder to the mod's name again.

## Updates

Mods you add with the **Add to game** button keep themselves up to date, and so does Recipe Browser, which comes
with Wax. About 20 seconds after the game starts, and every six hours after that, Wax asks the catalogue whether a
newer version is out. If one is, Wax downloads it, checks every file against its checksum and puts it in. A
notification tells you, for example "Recipe Browser was updated to 0.9.2."

- The mod's settings stay, and so does whether it is switched on or off.
- The version before is kept in the mods folder under a `.removed-` name. To go back, take the newer folder out
  and rename the kept one to the mod's name.
- A mod you wrote or copied into the mods folder yourself is never changed, even when the catalogue has a mod with
  the same name. Wax only updates a folder that holds a `wax.origin` file, which **Add to game** writes.
- A version that needs a newer Wax than yours is left out until you update Wax.

To decide yourself when a mod is updated, open the Wax menu (F8), go to **Mods** and switch off **Auto Update**.
Wax then only shows that a newer version is out: the mod's card has an **Update to** button. **Check now** asks
the catalogue at once.

Wax asks one address, `wax-icarus.duckdns.org`. It sends the name and version of the mod it fetches and its own
version, and nothing else.

This needs Wax 0.2.0 or newer, and a mod added with a Wax older than that has to be added once more. A mod you
installed from a zip is updated by extracting the new zip over the old folder.

## Vote on a mod

Every mod has two buttons with a thumb, one up and one down, on its card in the list and on its own page. Press
one to vote. Press it again to take your vote back, or press the other one to change it.

- You need no account. A vote is counted once for each internet address.
- The number beside each thumb is how many votes it has.
- Choose **Most liked** in the sort list to put the mods with the most votes up, less the votes down, first.

Windows made by mods are shown in **the menu**. The menu is open while anybody's windows are up.

- **F8** shows and hides the Wax panel.
- Every mod that has a window has a key of its own. That key shows and hides that mod's windows, and nobody
  else's.

So you can have the Wax panel up, or one mod's windows, or several at once.

## While the menu is open

- The windows of whoever you brought up are on screen.
- The mouse is free, so you can click.
- The game does not act on your keys or clicks. A click on a button does not also swing your axe.
- **Escape** closes the menu: everything that is up goes away. If a dropdown list is open, Escape closes that
  first.

Closing the last window that is up also closes the menu. Press the key again and the windows are back.

## A key for each mod

A mod's key is on its card of the **Mods** page of the Wax panel, as **Show / hide**.

- A mod can ask for a key. For a mod that asks for none, Wax picks the first of F6, F9, F10, F11, F5, F4, F3
  and F2 that nobody uses, and a notification tells you once which it is.
- To change it, click the key on the card and press the new one. It can be held with Ctrl, Shift or Alt.
- A key that somebody else already has is yours all the same. A notification says who else has it, and the
  card shows the clash until one of the two is changed.
- A mod can have more keys, each with a name ([`ui.Bind`](/docs/gui/hotkeys#let-the-player-choose-the-key)). They
  are on the same card and are changed the same way.
- When two mods act on one key, or a mod uses the key of the Wax panel, the card says so and a line at the top of
  the Mods page lists every clash. Change one of the keys and the line goes away.

[The Wax panel](/docs/debug-panel#a-key-for-each-mod) has more on this.

## Moving windows around

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Move a window | Drag its title bar. |
| Resize it | Drag any edge or any corner. The bottom right corner shows a grip. |
| Roll it up to its title bar | Click the minus button. Click the plus to roll it down. |
| Close it | Click the cross. |
| Make the page list wider | Drag the line between the list and the page. |

The mouse pointer changes over an edge or a corner, and that part of the window's outline lights up. It is
brighter while you hold it. The edge across from the one you drag stays where it is. A window does not get
smaller than it can be used at, and no edge goes past the edge of the screen.

Wax remembers each window's place and size the next time you play.

When the game's own window gets smaller, a window that would be out of reach is drawn back onto the screen.
Its own place is kept, so it goes back there when the game's window is large again.

## Things that are not part of the menu

[Overlays](/docs/gui/overlays) and [notifications](/docs/gui/notifications) show while you play. They never take the
mouse, and clicks go through them.

While the menu is open you can drag an overlay to a new place. Wax remembers that too.

## Changing the keys

For the Wax panel: open it, go to **Settings** and click the **Wax panel key** button. Then press the key you
want.

## The menu from code

`ui.Open`, `ui.Close` and `ui.Toggle` are about your own mod's windows. Other mods' windows and the Wax panel
stay as they are.

```lua title="init.lua"
local window = ui.Window({ title = "Menu", key = "F6" })

window:Field("My key", ui.Keys.Get() or "none")

window:Button("Hide my windows", function()
    ui.Close()
end)

ui.Opened:Connect(function()
    print("The menu opened.")
end)

ui.Closed:Connect(function()
    print("The menu closed.")
end)
```

| Function | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `ui.Open()` | Shows your mod's windows and frees the mouse. |
| `ui.Open({ windows = false })` | Frees the mouse and brings no window up. For a mod that shows [panels](/docs/gui/overlays#panels) only. |
| `ui.Close()` | Hides your mod's windows. The menu closes once nobody's windows are up. |
| `ui.Toggle()` | Shows them when they are away, hides them when they are up. |
| `ui.IsOpen()` | `true` while the menu is open, whoever opened it. |
| `ui.Window({ key = "F6" })` | The key your mod asks for. The first window your mod makes decides. |
| `ui.Opened`, `ui.Closed` | [Signals](/docs/signals) that fire when the menu opens and closes. |

### The keys

`ui.Keys` knows the key of everybody who has a window. An owner is `"Wax"` for the Wax panel, or a mod's id.

```lua title="init.lua"
local window = ui.Window({ title = "Keys", width = 320, height = 200 })

for _, owner in ipairs(ui.Keys.Owners()) do
    window:Field(owner.name, owner.key or "none")
end

ui.Keys.Changed:Connect(function(owner, key)
    print(owner, "now opens with", key or "no key")
end)
```

| Function | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `ui.Keys.Get(owner)` | The key of an owner. Leave `owner` out for your own mod. |
| `ui.Keys.Set(owner, key)` | Gives an owner another key, or none with `nil`. A key somebody has is given all the same: the second value is the name of who else has it, and a notification says so. |
| `ui.Keys.Owners()` | Everybody who has a window, Wax first. Each entry has `id`, `name`, `key` and `open`. |
| `ui.Keys.Changed` | A signal that fires with the owner and the new key. |
| `ui.GetToggleKey()`, `ui.SetToggleKey(key)` | The key of the Wax panel. The same as `ui.Keys.Get("Wax")` and `ui.Keys.Set("Wax", key)`. |
| `ui.KeyChanged` | A signal that fires with the new key of the Wax panel. |

Write `ui.Keys.Get` with a dot, not a colon.

A press that a [hotkey](/docs/gui/hotkeys) of your mod takes is not also taken as the key of your windows.

## Look without taking the mouse

`ui.SetPreview(true)` shows every window without freeing the mouse. You keep playing and can see the
windows. Use it for screenshots. `ui.SetPreview(false)` hides them again.

```lua
ui.SetPreview(true)
task.delay(3, function()
    ui.SetPreview(false)
end)
```

Every function is listed in the [ui reference](/docs/reference/ui#ui).

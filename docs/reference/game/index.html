game.Creatures:Spawn(kind, place?, options?)

Puts a new wild creature into the world with the game's own spawn. kind is a variant such as "Conifer_Wolf" or "Deer", or a kind that has one variant. A kind with several raises an error that lists them. place is a position, an actor or game.Me. When it is omitted the creature goes six metres in front of your character, and the options may stand in its place. Wax asks the game for ground a creature can walk on near the place, within 5 metres to the sides and at any height, and puts the creature a little above it. Where the game has none it raises an error and nothing is spawned. Inside a task Spawn waits until the game has given the creature its level, two seconds at most, and then answers the creature. Outside a task it answers at once, and Level and Health are right a few frames later. It answers nil and the reason when the creature left the world before it was finished. A tamed variant is refused in this version of Wax. The game's call took 3 to 4 ms when it was timed, and 18 ms once: a kind that is not in memory is loaded by the game inside the call. What is spawned stays when the mod unloads, unless the option keep is false.

ParameterType
kindstring
place (optional)WaxInstance | WaxMe | { X: number, Y: number, Z: number }
options (optional)WaxSpawnOptions

Returns

  • WaxCreature?
  • string?

Open in the Explorer

game.Creatures:SpawnMount(kind, place?, options?)

Puts a tamed animal into the world that is yours and can be ridden at once. kind is its set-up row such as "Mount_Moa" or "Mount_Horse", or a kind such as "Moa". It is made with the game's own spawn, named, given to you, and a saddle is put in its saddle slot, which is what makes the game give it a seat: after that the game's own "ride" works on it. place is a position or an actor. When it is omitted the mount comes a few metres in front of you, and the options may stand in its place. Where the game knows no ground to walk on under the place, as on a platform a mod built, the mount is made on the ground below and brought up. Inside a task it answers once the saddle is on, with the reason as a second value when the saddle did not go on. Outside a task it answers at once and finishes a moment later. The mount is saved with the prospect like any other.

instance:GetPropertyChangedSignal(name, seconds?)

A signal for one value of the object: a property of the game's that holds a number, true or false, text or an object, or a field Wax gives the class. It fires with (value, previous) when the value is found changed. The value is read ten times a second, and only while a handler is connected. An object arrives as an Instance, or nil when the property holds none. A table a field returns is compared by what it holds. seconds sets another time between reads, up to 3600. It is rounded to the nearest 0.05, and anything under 0.05 counts as 0.05. When one value is asked for at two paces, the faster is used. It works on an actor in the world and on a component that a property of its actor holds. Anything else raises an error that says why. When the object is gone, reading stops and every handler is disconnected. A task that waits on the signal is then not woken. A wrong name raises an error that suggests the right one.

ParameterType
namestring
seconds (optional)number

Returns WaxSignal<fun(value: any, previous: any)>

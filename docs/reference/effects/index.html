Effects and sounds

game.Effects and game.Sounds: particles, lights, trails, camera shakes and the game's sounds.

The Explorer searches everything on this page, and the classes of the game too.

game.Effects

game.Effects: the game's particle effects, lights, glowing trails and camera shakes, on anything. Everything a mod started is taken away when the mod unloads. Nothing costs anything while no effect is alive.

Type name: WaxEffects.

Open in the Explorer

game.Effects:Particles(path, options?)

Starts one of the game's particle effects on something.

ParameterTypeDescription
pathstringThe path of a particle effect of the game. game.Effects:Find looks for one.
options (optional)WaxParticleOptions

Returns WaxParticles

ParameterTypeDescription

WaxParticleOptions

What game.Effects:Particles takes besides the path.

Used by game.Effects:Particles.

Open in the Explorer

OptionTypeDescription
onanyWhat it hangs on: a character, a creature, a thing of a blueprint or a part. game.Me when omitted.
socketstringA bone or socket of what it hangs on, such as "bn_Prop_R_1" for the right hand of the player's arms.
at{ X: number, Y: number, Z: number } | number[]Where it sits, measured from what it hangs on.
turn{ Pitch?: number, Yaw?: number, Roll?: number }How it is turned, in degrees.
secondsnumber | falseHow long it stays. 3 when omitted. False keeps it until it is stopped.
settable<string, number|string|table>Parameters of the effect, by name: a number, a colour, or three numbers.

What game.Effects:Light takes.

Used by game.Effects:Light.

WaxTrailOptions

What game.Effects:Trail takes besides what it follows.

Used by game.Effects:Trail.

Open in the Explorer

OptionTypeDescription
from{ X: number, Y: number, Z: number } | number[]One end of the edge that leaves the trail, measured from what it follows.
to{ X: number, Y: number, Z: number } | number[]The other end. { 0, 0, 50 } when omitted.
colorstring | tableRed when omitted.
secondsnumberHow long the ribbon is, in time. 0.25 when omitted.
lifenumberHow long it runs. Until it is stopped when omitted.
widthnumberMakes it a streak this wide along the middle of the edge, always turned to face the view. Without it the ribbon lies between the edge's two ends, and a cut that comes straight at the view shows it from the side, as a line.
with_viewbooleanTrue keeps the trail with the view, as the arms of first person are, instead of leaving it behind in the world. For something held in first person.
delaynumberIt starts this many seconds from now. A swing winds up first, and a trail that only runs during the cut is one clean arc. life counts from when it starts.
embersnumber | { rate?: number, size?: number, life?: number, speed?: number, rise?: number }Small glowing bits that come off the edge while it moves: how many a second, or a table. They drift, rise and shrink to nothing. None when omitted.
materialWaxInstanceA material of your own in place of the plain glowing one, from game.Assets:Material. A thing held in first person is drawn bent toward the screen by its material, and a trail only lies on it when its material bends the same way.

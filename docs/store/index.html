The store

game.Workshop reads the store on the station, and changes its prices, the places of its nodes and what a node needs.

The game calls the store on the station the Workshop. game.Workshop reads it and can change parts of it.

Nothing here buys, researches or makes anything for the player.

How the store is built

  • A category is one screen of the store, such as the axes. Its Id is a name like "Workshop_Axes".
  • A node is one thing on that screen that can be researched and then made. Its Id is a name like "Workshop_Axe_Printed".
  • A node has two prices: Research, paid once, and Replicate, paid for each one made. A price is a table from a currency to an amount, such as { Credits = 100 }.
  • A node can come after other nodes. With several, one of them bought is enough.
  • A joint is a node of no size that shows nothing and sells nothing. It only joins lines.

Read it

FunctionWhat it gives
game.Workshop:Load()Reads the whole store ahead of time, a little each frame. Only inside a task.
game.Workshop:GetCategories()The categories, in the game's order.
game.Workshop:GetNodes(category)The nodes of one category, or of every category when you name none.
game.Workshop:GetNode(id)One node, or nil.
game.Workshop:GetCurrencies()The game's currencies.

Call Load first. Without it, the first GetNodes reads the whole store in one call, which holds the game up. With it, the store was read over 8 frames.

A node is a plain table. These are the fields you will use most:

FieldWhat it is
Id, CategoryIts name, and the Id of its category.
Name, IconThe name of what it sells and the path of its picture.
ItemThe item that is handed over, as game.Items names it.
Research, ReplicateIts two prices.
NeedsThe nodes it comes after.
At, SizeThe middle of the node on its category's screen, and how large it is.
Jointtrue for a joint.

GetNodes builds its list anew each time. Keep what it returns, and ask again when game.Workshop.Changed fires with "store".

Where the player stands

These answer while game.Workshop:IsReady() is true, which is in a prospect and not at the title screen.

FunctionWhat it gives
game.Workshop:GetState(id)"bought", "available" or "locked" for a node. What it costs is not part of the answer.
game.Workshop:GetBalance(currency)How many of a currency the account holds. Without a name, a table of them all.

game.Workshop.Changed fires with "player" when the player's side changed, and with "store" when the store's tables may read differently. Each of these calls asks the game, so call them when something is shown or pressed, not every frame.

Change a node

game.Workshop:Node(id) gives a node to change. Set takes a table with what should be different.

This node and this key are what was tried in the game: its research price was changed, and the store's screen showed the new price a moment later. at, needs, line and level were tried there too. replicate is written the same way and has not been looked at in the game yet.

In the tableWhat it changes
researchWhat researching the node costs. {} is a price of nothing.
replicateWhat each one made costs.
needsThe nodes it comes after: one name, or a list of which one bought is enough.
atThe middle of the node, such as { 1000, 1150 }.
lineHow the lines into the node are drawn: "elbow", "elbow-down", "straight" or "none".
levelThe level the node asks of the player.
  • Everything is checked first. A currency or a node the game does not have, or needs that could never be met, raise an error, and nothing is written.
  • Set gives back a list of warnings: a node it now lies on, a place outside the heights that fit the screen, and the like. An empty list when there is nothing.
  • The change belongs to your mod. It goes back when your mod unloads, or with suit:Reset(). game.Workshop:Reset() takes back everything your mod changed in the store.

Lay a category out

game.Workshop:Category(id):Arrange(shape, options) gives every node of a category a place in a shape: "line", "grid", "ring", "arc", "spiral", "tree" or "path".

The store's screen pans sideways only. A category can be as wide as you like, and its height has to fit the screen. game.Workshop.Layout has the shapes and the measures by themselves, for working places out without writing them.

What is switched off in this version

Each of these is there and raises an error that says so:

  • A category of your own. game.Workshop:AddCategory raises an error that says so. A node in one of the game's categories does work: see "A node of your own" below.
  • Hiding a node. node:Hide and node:Show.
  • A node that needs all of several nodes. The game only knows "one of".

A node of your own

game.Workshop:Define puts nodes into the game's categories. This one sells an item the mod added, under the sickles, after the Larkwell sickle:

  • The node shows with the item's own name and picture, its price, its lock and its line.
  • The game makes its store when the player arrives on the station and keeps the nodes it found then. When a node joins or leaves while the player is there, Wax has the game make its store again, so the node can be bought at once, and it is gone when the mod is switched off. The screen goes back to the category that was open.
  • What a player has researched is kept by the game, in the account, under the node's row name (<ModId>_<id>). So never change an id.
  • game.Workshop:Check(store) returns what is wrong with a description without putting anything in.

What cannot be undone

The store belongs to the account

What a player researches in the store is saved in their account, not in a character or a prospect. Wax pays nothing back and takes nothing away. If a player researches a node while your mod has made it cheap, it stays researched when the price goes back.

So say plainly on your mod's page what it changes in the store.

What has been seen

Prices, places, parents, line styles and levels were changed on the host of a prospect and read back from the game, and GetState followed a new parent and a new level from the next frame on. These have not been looked at yet:

  • The store's own screen with a change on it. It was never open while this was tried, so how new lines and the styles "straight" and "none" draw there is not known.
  • A game someone else hosts. A change is made in the tables on your PC only. The store is paid from each player's own account, so the other players are expected to keep their own store.

Every function is listed in The store reference.

Recipes

game.Recipes finds crafting recipes and changes what they take, what they give, how long they take and where they are made.

Assets

game.Assets loads the game's meshes, textures and materials by path, makes materials of your own, and builds shapes from numbers.
